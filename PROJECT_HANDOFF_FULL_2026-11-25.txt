======================================================================
2. WHAT THE PROJECT IS
======================================================================

`cartes_reduction` is an Odoo 17 addon for customer loyalty/reduction cards
and omnichannel sales. It extends an Odoo installation; it is not a
standalone service. It integrates with core Odoo Sales, Website/eCommerce,
Accounting, POS, portal/signup, loyalty, products, taxes, and payments.

Business goals:
- Manage four customer discount tiers and their active card assignments.
- Accrue loyalty points and promote customers using points or recent spend.
- Apply validated rewards in Website, Sales, and POS flows.
- Show a customer digital pass and tier progress in the portal.
- Operate a Tunisian furniture catalogue under Sotufab Distribution Tunisie.
- Produce French-facing invoice/email/storefront content.
- Provide customer RFM, CLV/churn, product association/Apriori analytics.
- Support controlled handling of sensitive CIN/customer fields and security
  audit functionality.

Main code areas in the addon:
- `models/`: cards, attribution, points, loyalty rules/rewards, partner
  extensions, Sales/POS integration, invoices, analytics, CIN/security/audit,
  dashboards, promotions, and wizards.
- `controllers/`: `/shop`, authentication, portal cards, public routes, and
  other website flows.
- `views/`: QWeb storefront/portal templates, backend forms, reports,
  dashboards, loyalty and security views.
- `static/src/js/`: website/POS/portal interactions, password policy.
- `static/src/scss/`: storefront and portal card styling.
- `static/src/xml/`: POS templates.
- `security/`: group definitions, access CSV, record rules.
- `data/`: seed/configuration XML.
- `migrations/`: versioned post-upgrade scripts.
- `tests/`: Odoo transactional and HTTP/browser-oriented tests.
- `ml_model/`: customer and product analytics/model code and datasets.
- `reports/`: printable QWeb reports.

Main Odoo manifest dependencies include `base`, `sale_management`, `website`,
`account`, `point_of_sale`, `loyalty`, `product`, `pos_loyalty`, `sale`,
`sale_loyalty`, `web`, `website_sale`, `portal`, `auth_signup`, and
`payment_demo`. Python extras include scikit-learn, joblib, pandas, mlxtend,
cryptography, and qrcode. Verify exact deployed manifest before changing
dependencies.

  `web.assets_frontend_minimal` script find the custom header and assume the

- The custom header no longer uses the native `id="top"`. A regression
  assertion was added to `tests/test_ui_security_polish.py`.
- This QWeb update was upgraded into production.
